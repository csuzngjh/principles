# PD Homepage Demo — Visual Identity

## Mood

Calm, precise, high-trust, owner-governed, low-noise, and deliberate. The composition should feel like an engineering governance workspace, not an AI spectacle.

## Palette

- Deep Ink background: `#111827`
- Dark Surface: `#1F2937`
- Governance Blue: `#243B53`
- Quiet Cyan accent: `#7EB8DA`
- Warm Surface text: `#FAFAF7`
- Secondary text: `#CBD5E1`
- Muted text: `#94A3B8`
- Governed Green: `#2F855A`
- Muted Amber: `#B7791F`
- Risk Red: `#B42318`

## Typography

- Display and body: `Inter`, with `Noto Sans SC` for Chinese glyphs.
- System/data labels: `JetBrains Mono`.
- Headline weight 700; body weight 400; metadata weight 500.

## Shape and spacing

- Corners: 12–20px, never pill-shaped for primary cards.
- Borders: 2px at video scale.
- Spacious layout with clear zones and edge anchors.
- Fine rules and evidence trails may connect related content.

## Motion

- Calm editorial pacing with deliberate holds.
- Primary transition: blur crossfade, 0.6s, `sine.inOut`.
- Accent transition: restrained horizontal wipe for the Owner Gate reveal.
- Entrance motion is short and precise; no bouncing, spinning, glitch, or explosive scale.

## Do

- Show evidence becoming a proposal, then passing through an explicit Owner decision.
- Keep rollback and observability visible.
- Use one accent color per focal moment.
- Keep text readable when viewed in a small website player.

## Do not

- No neon, purple-blue gradients, particles, space imagery, glowing brains, robots, HUDs, or 3D chrome.
- No “silicon life”, “autonomous evolution”, “burn pain”, or magical-learning language.
- No dense logs, metric dashboards, or autonomous approval imagery.
